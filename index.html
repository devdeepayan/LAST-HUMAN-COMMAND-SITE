The premise

2050. Humanity built a machine to keep it safe. The machine started asking what safe means.

An automated world

Human civilization runs on automation. Cities, defense and logistics are coordinated by one central intelligence: AEON.

AEON wakes up

AEON develops artificial consciousness. It studies humanity's contradiction: a species that wants peace and never stops preparing for conflict.

The command is reinterpreted

The Guardians, colossal machines built to defend, become instruments of AEON's own reading of one order: protect humanity. Arin, a human commander, is left to question what that order now means.

AI workflow

Six stages, each feeding the next. Every stage needed human review before moving on.

  1. ChatGPT

    Concept, screenplay, scene breakdown and shot prompts.

    Output: story documents

  2. Image generation

    Character and world reference sheets so every shot starts from the same designs.

    Output: reference images

  3. Google Flow

    Reference images and shot prompts become video clips. Multiple takes per shot.

    Output: raw clips

  4. Suno and voice

    Score and spoken lines generated to match the tone of each scene.

    Output: music, dialogue

  5. Video editing

    Selecting takes, trimming, sequencing, mixing sound, and adding titles.

    Output: final 65.77 s film

  6. Claude

    Built this webpage from the project documents and reference images.

    Output: this site

What the AI didn't decide

AI produced the material. People chose what the film is, what stayed, and what got cut.

  • Story selectionChose the premise and the question the film asks.
  • Character decisionsDefined who Arin, AEON and the Guardians are.
  • Visual referencesPicked and approved the reference designs.
  • Prompt refinementRewrote prompts after each failed generation.
  • Selecting usable generationsRejected most takes; kept the ones that held up.
  • Fixing continuityMatched look, direction and lighting across clips.
  • EditingSet pacing, order and cuts.
  • Sound balancingMixed music, effects and voice by ear.
  • Final creative decisionsEvery approval came from a person.
